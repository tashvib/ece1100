Career Goals

Short-term (next 1–3 years)

Build hardware skills and get industry experience

  • Land a Summer 2027 internship in AI/ML hardware acceleration, digital design, or explainable AI.
  • Strengthen my foundations through ECE coursework in computer architecture, GPU programming, and hardware/software co-design for AI, with a focus on how AI workloads run on chips.
  • Join the AI in the Era of Supercomputing team through Georgia Tech's Vertically Integrated Projects (VIP) program.
  • Get more involved in Georgia Tech technical organizations, including Silicon Jackets (digital chip design), AI@GT (applied AI research), and Supercomputing at GT (high-performance computing).
Long-term (5–10+ years)

Build industry experience, then build something of my own

  • Work as an ML framework engineer optimizing how large language models run on GPUs and custom silicon, where AI software and chip hardware meet.
  • Learn firsthand how hardware and AI products are designed, built, and brought to market, and grow a network of engineers and mentors.
  • Use that foundation to create something that helps people, either by engineering a new invention or by founding a startup in an area I am passionate about.
  • Keep making technology accessible by mentoring students and supporting education in under-resourced communities.

Why build something of my own?

Inventors and founders often describe the same motivation: owning a problem from start to finish, moving quickly from idea to working product, and seeing their work make a direct difference in people's lives. That is what draws me too. Building GreenOvation showed me how rewarding it is to take an idea from a sketch to an app that more than 100 people used. I want to spend the first part of my career learning how great hardware and AI products are built at scale, and then use that experience to create something of my own, whether that is an invention I engineer or a company I start around a problem I care about.
